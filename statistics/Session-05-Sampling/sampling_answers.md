## Task 3: Why Use Sampling on Swiggy?

Collecting delivery times for every Swiggy order would require a lot of time and resources. Instead, Swiggy can select a representative sample of orders from different cities, restaurants, and time periods to estimate delivery performance efficiently.

## Task 4: Sampling Bias

This scenario introduces selection bias because the survey is sent only to users who recently streamed Bollywood songs. Users who prefer other music genres or are inactive are excluded, so the results may not represent all Spotify users.

## Task 5: Sampling Methods for BookMyShow

### 1. Random Sampling
Randomly select 500 users from BookMyShow's user list and study their booking frequency, preferred movie genres, and booking times.

### 2. Stratified Sampling
Divide users into groups based on age or city. Then randomly select users from each group to ensure different groups are represented in the survey.
