## Task 1: Playlist Duration

Playlist song durations:

[210, 180, 245, 200, 175] seconds

Total duration:

210 + 180 + 245 + 200 + 175 = 1010 seconds

Total duration in minutes:

1010 / 60 = 16.83 minutes

**Answer:** The total playlist duration is 1010 seconds, or approximately 16.83 minutes.
