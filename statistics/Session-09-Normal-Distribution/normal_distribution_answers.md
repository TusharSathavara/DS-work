# Session 9 - Normal Distribution

## Task 1: Generate Random Numbers

Python's NumPy library generates 100 random values from a normal distribution with a mean of 50 and a standard deviation of 10.

A histogram and bell curve are plotted using Matplotlib.

Because the values are randomly generated, their sample mean and standard deviation may not be exactly 50 and 10.

## Task 2: Daily Step Counts

Dataset:

[5000, 6200, 7000, 6500, 8000, 7500, 6800, 7200, 6000, 7100, 6900, 7300, 6400, 7600, 7000, 6700, 7400, 6600, 7100, 7200]

- Mean: Approximately 6905
- Median: Approximately 7050

The mean, median, and mode are not exactly equal. In a perfectly symmetrical normal distribution, these measures are expected to be equal, but real samples may differ.

## Task 3: Online Game Scores

The scores of 200 players are simulated using a normal distribution with:

- Mean: 70
- Standard deviation: 15

One standard deviation from the mean is the range from 55 to 85.

According to the 68-95-99.7 rule, approximately 68% of values in a normal distribution fall within one standard deviation of the mean.

The simulated percentage may vary due to random sampling.

## Task 4: Real-World Example

Example: Food delivery times.

Delivery times may be approximately normally distributed when orders are collected under similar conditions. Most deliveries may take close to the average time, while fewer take much less or much more time.

However, delays, traffic, weather, and cancellations can make actual delivery times skewed. Therefore, the normal distribution should be checked against real data before using it.
