# Session 8 - Probability Basics

## Task 1: Probability of Getting Heads

A fair coin has two possible outcomes: Heads and Tails.

- Favourable outcomes: 1
- Total outcomes: 2
- Probability of Heads: 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%

## Task 2: Dice Simulation

A six-sided die is rolled 100 times using Python's random module.

The number of times 6 appears is counted. Experimental probability is calculated as:

Experimental Probability = Number of times 6 appears / Total rolls

The theoretical probability of getting a 6 is 1/6, approximately 0.167.

The experimental probability may differ because of random variation.

## Task 3: Probability of a Pop Song

The example dataset contains 20 songs, including 10 Pop songs.

Probability of Pop = 10 / 20 = 0.5 = 50%

Therefore, the probability of randomly selecting a Pop song from this dataset is 50%.

## Task 4: Food and Dessert Orders

Food orders: U1, U2, U3, U4, U5

Dessert orders: U2, U3, U6

Users who ordered both food and dessert: U2, U3

Total unique users: 6

Probability of both = 2 / 6 = 0.3333, or approximately 33.33%.

## Task 5: Conditional Probability

Suppose 100 users bought a phone, and 35 of those users also bought headphones.

P(Headphones | Phone) = Users who bought both / Users who bought a phone

P(Headphones | Phone) = 35 / 100 = 0.35 = 35%

A shopping app can use this information to recommend headphones to users who purchase a phone.
