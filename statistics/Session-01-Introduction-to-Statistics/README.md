# Session 1 - Introduction to Statistics in Data Science

## Task 1: Daily Actions and Types of Data

Here are five daily actions performed on apps and the types of data they generate.

| No. | App | Daily Action | Data Generated | Data Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Instagram | Checking the number of likes on a post | 150 likes | Numerical |
| 2 | Instagram | Selecting a post category | Travel | Categorical |
| 3 | Zomato | Rating a restaurant | 4.5 stars | Numerical |
| 4 | Flipkart | Selecting a product category | Electronics | Categorical |
| 5 | Spotify | Listening to songs for a certain duration | 30 minutes | Numerical |

Numerical data represents quantities that can be measured or counted. Categorical data represents labels or groups, such as music genres or product categories.

## Task 2: Classifying Music App Data

**Answer: Categorical Nominal Data**

The data is categorical because it represents music genres rather than numerical measurements. It is nominal data because the genres have no natural order or ranking.

## Task 3: How Statistics Can Improve an App Feature

**App:** Zomato

**Feature:** Restaurant Ratings

Statistics can help Zomato analyze restaurant ratings and understand customer satisfaction. For example, the average rating can show the overall performance of a restaurant, while the number of ratings can indicate how many customers reviewed it. Comparing ratings over time can help identify restaurants whose service or food quality is improving or declining.

This information can help users make better restaurant choices and help restaurants improve their services.

## Task 4: Three Statistical Terms for Playlist Recommendations

### 1. Mean

Mean is the average of a set of numerical values.

**Example:** If a user listens to music for 20, 30, and 40 minutes over three days, the mean listening time is 30 minutes per day.

### 2. Median

Median is the middle value when numerical data is arranged in order.

**Example:** If a user listens to songs for 10, 15, and 60 minutes, the median listening time is 15 minutes. It helps describe typical listening behaviour when one day has an unusually high listening time.

### 3. Mode

Mode is the most frequently occurring value in a dataset.

**Example:** If a user listens to Pop, Rock, Pop, Jazz, and Pop songs, the mode is Pop because it appears most frequently.

These statistical terms can help a music app understand listening habits and recommend songs that better match a user's preferences.
