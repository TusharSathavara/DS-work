## Task 1: Daily Actions and Types of Data

Here are five daily actions performed on apps and the types of data they generate.

| No. | App | Daily Action | Data Generated | Data Type |

| 1 | Instagram | Checking likes on a post | 150 likes | Numerical |

**App:** Zomato

Statistics can help Zomato analyze customer ratings and understand restaurant performance. ## Task 4: Three Statistical Terms for Playlist Recommendations

### 1. Mean

Mean is the average of a set of numerical values.

**Example:** If a user listens to music for 20, 30, and 40 minutes over three days, the mean listening time is 30 minutes per day.

### 2. Median

Median is the middle value when numerical data is arranged in ascending order.

**Example:** If a user listens for 10, 15, and 60 minutes, the median listening time is 15 minutes. It helps describe typical listening behaviour when one day has unusually high listening time.

### 3. Mode

Mode is the most frequently occurring value in a dataset.

**Example:** If a user listens to Pop, Rock, Pop, Jazz, and Pop songs, the mode is Pop because it appears most frequently.

These statistical terms can help a music app understand listening habits and recommend songs that better match users' preferences.
